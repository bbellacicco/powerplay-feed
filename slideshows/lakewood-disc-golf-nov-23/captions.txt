photo2.jpg | Lights on at Lakewood | The Windsor Essex Disc Golf Club has put a solar light on every basket at Lakewood Park in Tecumseh. The lights come on by themselves and last well after dark, ahead of the park's 10 p.m. curfew.
photo1.jpg | No more flashlights | "It was a bit of a pain before because when people play, they would have to bring their own light, walk up to the basket, put it on, walk back to the tee," said Ron Hebert, co-founder of the Windsor Essex Disc Golf Club. "Especially during league, when they had glow league, it got so cumbersome. So the club decided to invest in that."
photo3.jpg | Twinkling lights | About a dozen tee posts also got solar caps, with the OK from the town's parks staff. "Even when I'm driving down Manning, I can look out and I see all these twinkling lights," Hebert said. The club is hoping to light all 18 posts next season.
photo4.jpg | Glow nights | A regular group plays glow disc golf at Lakewood every Tuesday, and some are out on Thursdays too. "It is nice to see people up there throwing the glow disc," Hebert said.

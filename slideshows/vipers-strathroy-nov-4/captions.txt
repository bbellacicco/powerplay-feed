title: Game Day Snaps: Captain Chartrand returns as Vipers edge Rockets
credit: Powerplay Windsor
date: 2026-11-04

photo1.jpg | The captain is back | LaSalle Vipers captain Connor Chartrand (17) was back in the lineup Wednesday night after missing three weeks with a shoulder injury, and he scored the game's first goal.
photo2.jpg | 'Everyone just worked together' | The Vipers edged the Strathroy Rockets 3-2 at the Vollmer Complex. "It was a great game from the guys. Everyone just worked together, executed the game plan and came out on top tonight," Chartrand told PowerplayWindsor.com. "Got more work to do."
photo3.jpg | 'Everyone helping out' | Chartrand said the sprain to his AC joint was mild, with a recovery time of three to six weeks. "It was good to get one on the board for the guys, but that was everyone helping out," he said.
photo4.jpg | 'Points are important' | The win moves LaSalle to 7-8 with 14 points, eighth in the GOHL's Western Conference and one point back of London and Strathroy. "Nothing I like more than winning. Nothing I hate more than losing," Chartrand said. Next up: the Elmira Sugar Kings on Saturday at the Vollmer Complex, 7 p.m.
